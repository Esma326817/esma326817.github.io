🧠 What is HINT?

HINT (Human-Intent Inception) is an innovative application that lets you guide robots through complex, long-horizon tasks using natural human instructions. Instead of programming every tiny step, you simply tell the robot your goal, and HINT handles the complicated planning.

Why People Love HINT:

  • 🕹️ Extremely Easy Setup - Download, run, and you're ready in minutes
  • 🗣️ Natural Language Control - Use everyday words to command robots
  • 🔮 Smart Planning - HINT automatically breaks down big tasks into steps
  • 🔄 Adaptive Behavior - Adjusts to unexpected situations automatically
  • 📊 Clear Progress Tracking - See what the robot is doing at all times
  • 🛡️ Safe & Reliable - Built with safety-first logic

Perfect for: hobbyists, students, researchers, and professionals who want to explore robotics without diving into complex programming.

⚙️ How Does It Work?

Step 1: Understand

Tell HINT your goal in plain language. For example: "Pick up the red cup and place it next to the blue bowl."

Step 2: Plan

HINT's smart algorithm breaks down your request into a detailed step-by-step plan the robot can follow.

Step 3: Execute

The robot carries out each step while HINT monitors progress and makes adjustments if anything unexpected happens.
